How It Works

From enquiry to running water

01

Enquiry & Site Visit

Contact us via WhatsApp or Call. We discuss your site and arrange a visit to assess access and likely drilling depth.

02

Quote Confirmation

Using the depth-based price guide, we confirm your total cost - including any extra steel casing if your site needs it.

03

Drilling & Casing

Our rig drills to the agreed depth and the borehole is lined with PVC casing (or steel casing, where selected).

04

Pump Installation & Handover

A submersible pump matched to your depth is installed and tested, and the completed borehole is handed over to you.
